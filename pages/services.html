How we work

Six calm steps to your wedding day

A clear, proven process so you always know what happens next — and who is looking after it.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A relaxed first chat about your story, priorities, budget and the feel you want.

  2. 02

    Concept & mood board

    We shape a design direction with colours, florals, textures and a clear plan.

  3. 03

    Vendor selection

    We shortlist trusted vendors that match your style and budget, then book them.

  4. 04

    Detailed planning

    Timelines, floor plans, run-of-show and every booking confirmed in writing.

  5. 05

    Decor setup

    Our crew installs the stage, florals, lighting and tables while you get ready.

  6. 06

    coordination

    We run the day from first call to last song, so you can simply be present.
