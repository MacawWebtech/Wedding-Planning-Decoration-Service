Our story

About our studio

Who we are

Planners, designers and very good listeners

We started with one simple belief: planning a wedding should feel exciting, not exhausting.

Since 20XX we have helped couples plan intimate ceremonies, big family celebrations and weddings far from home. We bring planning, decor, florals and coordination together, so you have one team and one plan.

Every wedding gets a lead planner, a clear timeline and an honest budget. You will always know what is happening, what it costs and who is looking after it.
